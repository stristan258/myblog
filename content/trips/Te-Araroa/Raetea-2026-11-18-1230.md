This was a pretty brutal day of stomping. We set off from the Krishna Sanctuary at around 6AM in the drizzling rain and didn’t make it to camp by until around 5.30PM. Clocking up 22km, with 1,700m elevation gain. 

The track through the forest took us along a ridge line with lots of ups and downs, but the challenging part was the combination of deep mud mixed with crisscrossing roots. At points the mud was knee-height. This started off being quite fun, but the novelty wore off after hour 10 passed and the rain intensified. 

Probably one of the most challenging days hiking I’ve experienced, which is unexpected for forest track. Let’s see if this is still the case in a couple of months’ time!
