My location, sent live from a Garmin inReach satellite messenger.

### How to view it

1. Tap the link above. It opens my Garmin MapShare page.
2. Enter the passcode when asked. If you don't have it, [request it by email](mailto:stristan258@gmail.com?subject=Map%20share%20passcode&body=Hi%20Tristan%2C%20could%20you%20send%20me%20the%20map%20share%20passcode%3F).
3. The map shows my latest known position. Use the date controls on the page to look back at earlier days.

A few things to know:

- A new point is sent about every 10 minutes while I'm moving. When I'm stopped (lunch, camp), updates are much less frequent, so a long gap isn't a worry.
- Points need a clear view of the sky. Dense forest or deep valleys can cause gaps.
- I may switch the device off overnight, so the map may show nothing until I start walking again.
